Modules

The zone of the modules

On the right, the zone of the modules has the palette module: medium for the zone, deep for the titles of the modules, light for their content. A module may then give its content a palette of its own.

The palette editor is a module of an application: its content is the real one, its frame is simpler here. Its settings are configuration items, kept in memory in this demo: they are lost when the page is loaded again.

Move the hue and the intensity, switch day and night, show the states: the preview follows. Then copy the CSS rule into the CSS of your application, and give the palette to a block: <section palette="game">. See the guide of the themes.
