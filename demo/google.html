Ricochet — Google account

Connection and requests

An application without any server reaches the Drive and the Tasks of its user: the token of the account is kept in this browser (secrets), renewed at a click in its last minutes, and asked again by « Reconnect » once expired. The client identifier comes from a project of the Google Cloud console whose authorized JavaScript origins include the address of this page. See src/adapters/google.mjs and demo/google.mjs.
